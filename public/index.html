How to play
  1. Each player arranges their hand face down in ascending order (aces can go anywhere). Red cards show sideways, black cards upright, so everyone can see the colours but not the ranks.
  2. On your turn (teams mode) your partner may privately show you one of their cards.
  3. Pick one opponent's face-down card and guess its rank. Right: it flips face up and you guess again. Wrong: nothing happens to your cards, the turn just passes.
  4. The first player (or team) to have every opponent card face up wins the round.
