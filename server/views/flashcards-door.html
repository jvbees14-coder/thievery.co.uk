Flashcards

Write a card. The house appraises it, stamps it and mints it. Print it on real card, or lay it on the table and trade it blind for whatever comes back — different cards, same worth.

3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers, dots, dashes, underscores.

At least 10 characters. Length beats punctuation.

Three cards are dealt to a new account, so you arrive with something to trade.
