    Elevation

    Every shadow is layered and tinted toward the page. In the dark, a raised surface also catches a line of light along its top edge (--edge-light).

    --shadow-1 · resting
    --shadow-2 · raised
    --shadow-3 · floating

    Motion

    Interface movement is 120 to 280ms and never eases in. Press the button to run them; press and hold it to feel the give.

    quick · 120 · out
    base · 200 · out
    slow · 280 · out
    slow · 280 · move
