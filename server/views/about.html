About

Thievery started as a single card game, made to play with friends: a deduction game where everyone can see the colour of your cards but only you know what they are. The site has picked up a few more rooms since. There are flashcards to write and trade, Battle for quizzing each other on them, and Switchhead, a shedding game where the hands move under you.

It’s an independent project, made in the UK. There are no ads and no trackers, and the card game works without an account and always will.
