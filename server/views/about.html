About

I made Thievery so my friends and I had a card game we couldn’t find anywhere else: one where everyone can see the colour of your cards but only you know what they are, and the whole game is working out the rest before they work out yours. Once it was up, other things followed. Flashcards to write and trade, Battle for quizzing each other on them, Switchhead for when you want the hands to move under you, and Mind maps for when you’d rather think than play.

It’s one person’s project, made in the UK and kept free. There are no ads and no trackers, and the card game works without an account and always will. If something breaks, or you’ve got an idea for a game, I’d like to hear it.

The name is a joke. The only thing stolen here is cards, from your friends, in a game, and they get them back at the end of the round.
