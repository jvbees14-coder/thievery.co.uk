First, put this on your Home Screen

iPhones only let a website send notifications once it’s on the Home Screen. It takes a few taps.

  1. 1 Tap Share at the bottom of Safari (top right on an iPad).
  2. 2 Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen and then Add.
  3. 3 Close Safari and open Missions from its new icon on your Home Screen.
  4. 4 Tap Join, then Allow when your phone asks about notifications.

Needs iOS 16.4 or later. On an older iPhone you can still join here and check this page for missions.
