Switchhead

Get rid of your cards, and do not be the last one holding any. But the hands swap when nobody is looking, and every so often the goal turns over — go out then, and you go out at the bottom.

A room is four characters and lives in memory only. Share the code; a code for a game already under way lets you watch it and sits you down for the next one.

How it plays

  • Three cards face down, three face up on top of them, three in your hand. Before the first card you may swap cards between your hand and your face-up row.
  • Play a card as high as the one on the pile or higher — several of one rank at once if you have them — and draw back up to three while the deck lasts. Cannot, or would rather not? Pick up the pile.
  • When your hand is gone, play from your face-up row; when that is gone, turn the face-down cards over one at a time and hope.
  • The swap. Every few seconds there is an even chance two players’ hands trade places. Nothing warns you.
  • The goal. Every five to fifteen turns it turns over. While it says win, going out takes the best place left. While it says lose, going out takes the worst.

The special cards

The first three are always in play. The host adds any of the rest.

Only the host picks the cards and deals.
