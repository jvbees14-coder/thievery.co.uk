Switchhead

Get rid of your cards, and don’t be the last one holding any. But the hands swap when nobody’s looking, and sometimes the goal flips: go out then, and you finish last.

For 2 to 8 players. Share the room code with friends. If a game has already started, you can watch and join the next one.

How it plays

  • Three cards face down, three face up on top of them, three in your hand. Before the first card you may swap cards between your hand and your face-up row.
  • Play a card at least as high as the one on the pile (several of one rank at once, if you have them) and draw back up to three while the deck lasts. Can’t, or don’t want to? Pick up the pile.
  • When your hand is gone, play from your face-up row; when that is gone, turn the face-down cards over one at a time and hope.
  • The swap. Every ten to fifteen turns two players’ hands trade places. Nothing warns you.
  • The goal. Every ten to fifteen turns it flips. While it says win, going out takes the best place left. While it says lose, going out takes the worst.

The special cards

The first three are always in play. The host adds any of the rest.

Only the host picks the cards and deals.
