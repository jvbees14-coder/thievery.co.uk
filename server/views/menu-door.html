A free card game for 2 to 12 players

Steal their cards before they steal yours.

Thievery is a deduction card game you play in the browser. Everyone keeps a hand face down, in order. On your turn, point at a card in someone else’s hand and name it. Get it right and it flips. Flip all of theirs before they flip all of yours.
