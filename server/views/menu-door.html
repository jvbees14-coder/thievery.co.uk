A free card game for 2 to 12 players

Steal their cards before they steal yours.

Thievery is a deduction card game you play in the browser. Everyone arranges a hand face down, in order. On your turn you point at somebody else’s hidden card and name it. Get it right and it flips. Flip all of theirs before they flip all of yours.
